A deviation investigation, conducted in minutes.

What you're watching: the agent opens a deviation record in the QMS, pulls the batch record and process parameters for that lot from the MES, pulls the associated release and in-process results from the LIMS, then lines the lot up against comparable batches and returns a timeline with the candidate causes ranked and the evidence for each one cited back to its source record. The agent prepares a draft report, including findings, RCA, CAPAs and conclusion. Ready for human review.
