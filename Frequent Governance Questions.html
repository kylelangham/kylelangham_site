Frequent Compliance/Governance Questions

Frequently Asked Questions regarding project delivery governance and compliance.
How does an agentic system ensure privacy and confidentiality?Privacy / Confidentiality
Robust data controls are enforced at the data ingestion layer. An agentic system should not be able to access PII, trade secrets or confidential data, as identified by the client.
What can be validated?QUALITY / COMPLIANCE
Much of an agentic system can be validated, typically including data ingestion, transformation, semantics, evaluation, and verification. Ultimately, the LLM part of the system can not be validated and as such any output from an agentic system will require proceduralized human review and approval for GxP operations.
Are Part 11 records or data integrity controls affected?Quality / Compliance
No records are created, altered, or deleted in any system of record, ever. Access is read-only throughout. ALCOA+ posture is unchanged by this work.
Where does our data go, and does it leave our control?IT Security
Default position is that no proprietary, PII or confidential data leaves client systems (see privacy / confidential data). Any data that an agentic system can access is assumed to be available for the client's choosen LLM model and subject to those Terms of Service.
What happens when an AI output is wrong?Quality / IT
Best practices calls for evaluation and verification scripts for every agentic AI output. However, human oversight, review and approval is needed for any quality output from an agentic system.
Who owns this after the vendor leaves?IT
For any build, ownership, support model, and internal skill transfer get defined before the build starts rather than after. If we cannot name an internal owner at that point, that is a reason not to build. Vendor support will be available per agreement.
Does this lock us into a vendor or a platform?IT / Procurement
Good agentic systems should be vendor-neutral, built with transferable primitives. However, some data systems may require additional licensing at the client's expense.
