What it is

A fixed-scope assessment of whether your data and systems are ready for the specific business initiative you're about to fund. Delivered as six concrete artifacts and a costed roadmap, for one flat fee agreed before we start.

What you get
  • 1
    System & data-flow inventory
    Every system, what data lives where, on what medium (spreadsheet, SaaS, or validated system)
  • 2
    Data-quality scorecard
    Rated across completeness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness, and integrity
  • 3
    Maturity assessment
    Where you are today versus where your initiative requires you to be
  • 4
    Gap & risk register
    Every gap scored by severity and effort for sequencing
  • 5
    Written assessment report
    Findings, evidence, and the reasoning behind each
  • 6
    Costed roadmap
    Priced with your named initiative as the destination
What I do to produce it

  • Inventory

    Map the systems and analyze data ingestion limitations.

  • Quality

    Profile how good that data actually is against the standard integrity dimensions.

  • Flow & Ownership

    Trace how data moves across handoffs and establish who owns and controls each dataset.

  • Readiness verdict

    Measure the gap between today and your named target state and price the path to close it.

How scope is set
Systems in scope
We agree the list of systems up front. That list sets the boundary and prevents scope creep.
The initiative you name
The target state you're funding (an AI/agentic build, a system migration, a transfer, ...). This sets what "ready" means and where the assessment goes deep.

The same rigorous data first principles will be applied regardless of scope and initiative.

What it is not
  • Not a software purchase or a tool implementation
  • Not a vendor pitch: findings are system-agnostic and I sell you nothing downstream you don't choose
  • Not open-ended consulting: fixed scope, fixed fee, fixed timeline
  • Not a re-litigation of decisions already made: it de-risks the path you've chosen
